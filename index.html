From footstep to electricity in four moves.

A purely mechanical chain of springs, gears and a DC motor, built into a modular tile.

01

Downward impact

A pedestrian footstep triggers a smooth downward movement of the tile.

02

Reverse Scotch yoke

Converts the linear downward displacement directly into rotational torque.

03

Compound gear

A compound gear series multiplies rotational speed exponentially.

04

DC generation

High-speed rotation spins the DC motor to produce electrical energy.
